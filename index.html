How to play

Steer the snake with arrow keys / WASD (or swipe).
Eat the glowing letters to spell a crossword answer in order (they stick to your body).
Wrong letters get spat out (−5). Letters already filled in by crossing words are supplied for you.
Finish a word and it locks into the puzzle. Hit a wall or yourself and it's over. Fill the whole crossword to win!
